# 🏭 1-CLICK INDUSTRY PRESET TEMPLATES BLUEPRINT

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target:** Ready-Made Niche Blueprints for Instant 1-Click Client Onboarding  

---

## 1. Education & Coaching Institutes (`preset_coaching`)
- **Welcome Trigger:** Asks for student name, target exam (NEET / JEE / UPSC), and current class.
- **Auto-Assets:** PDF Prospectus, Fee Structure, Faculty Testimonials.
- **Interactive Buttons:** `[ 📄 Download Prospectus ]`, `[ 📅 Book Free Demo Class ]`, `[ 📞 Talk to Counselor ]`.
- **Abandonment Trigger:** Follow-up 24h later offering a ₹1,000 Early Bird Scholarship.

---

## 2. Solar Energy & Rooftop Installers (`preset_solar`)
- **Welcome Trigger:** Asks for monthly electricity bill amount (₹1,500 - ₹10,000+).
- **Auto-Assets:** Government Subsidy Calculator breakdown (PM Surya Ghar Scheme: ₹78,000 subsidy chart).
- **Interactive Buttons:** `[ ☀️ Calculate Subsidy ]`, `[ 📍 Book Free Roof Inspection ]`, `[ 📄 View Past Installations ]`.
- **Payment Action:** Collects ₹500 refundable roof survey token via UPI.

---

## 3. Dental & Medical Clinics (`preset_dental_clinic`)
- **Welcome Trigger:** Asks for ailment (Tooth Pain, Implants, Braces, Routine Checkup).
- **Auto-Assets:** Doctor qualifications, Clinic timings, Location Map Pin.
- **Interactive Buttons:** `[ 🩺 Book Appointment Slot ]`, `[ 📍 Get Google Map Route ]`, `[ 💰 Price List ]`.
- **Reminder Trigger:** WhatsApp Reminder 2 hours before scheduled consultation.

---

## 4. Real Estate & Property Dealers (`preset_real_estate`)
- **Welcome Trigger:** Asks for budget (₹30L – ₹1Cr+) and configuration (2BHK, 3BHK, Commercial).
- **Auto-Assets:** HD Floor plans, Video Walkthrough links, Price Sheets.
- **Interactive Buttons:** `[ 🏠 View Floor Plans ]`, `[ 🚗 Book Free Site Visit Cab ]`, `[ 📞 Speak to Property Advisor ]`.

---

## 5. D2C / E-Commerce Retail (`preset_ecommerce`)
- **Welcome Trigger:** Shows product categories & top-selling discount items.
- **Auto-Assets:** WhatsApp Interactive Product Catalog.
- **Interactive Buttons:** `[ 🛍️ Browse Catalog ]`, `[ 🎟️ Get 15% Off Coupon ]`, `[ 📦 Track My Order ]`.
- **Cart Recovery:** Triggers WhatsApp message 1 hour after checkout abandonment with a direct payment link.
